*Center for Astrophysics, Harvard & Smithsonian*, **April 4, 12:00 pm**

### How Astronomy and Physics Majors Do Great Things: IXPE Overview and Systems Engineering Perspective

I will discuss the design, build and launch of the Imaging X-ray Polarimetry Explorer (IXPE) and describe how its development was uniquely enabled by multiple partners. I will discuss my own experience bringing the mission together as the lead systems engineer, and how a systems engineering career may be suitable for physics & astronomy majors.
